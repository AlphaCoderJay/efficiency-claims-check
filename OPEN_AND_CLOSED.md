# What is open, and what is not

DotrixAI works in the open and also has a small commercial side. We keep the two apart so that contributors always know where their work goes.

## Open: everything in this repository

- The charter, the claim list, proposals, technique notes, standards, the method, the templates and the harness.
- Every check submitted here, with its code, logs and verdict.
- Corrections, including corrections of our own mistakes.

Contributors keep credit for their work. Results are published under CC BY 4.0 and code under MIT, so anyone, including DotrixAI, may use them under those licenses.

## Not here: DotrixAI's commercial work

DotrixAI licenses evaluation tasks for AI research agents. They are built from the record of our own research program, CIR, and some depend on experimental outcomes we have not published. Those tasks and their answers are confidential and are not part of this repository.

The rules that keep this separation honest:

1. **Nothing confidential enters this repository.** No withheld CIR outcomes, no task answers.
2. **Nothing from this repository is sold as exclusive.** Open results stay open. We will not sell a contributor's work, and we will not use it to build confidential task sets.
3. **Our own checks here are public like everyone else's.** If DotrixAI runs a check in this project, its pre-registration and result are published here.
4. **Conflicts are stated.** If a claim on the list relates to our commercial work, we say so in the claim row.
