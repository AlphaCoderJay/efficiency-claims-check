## Checklist

For a pre-registration:
- [ ] The claim or proposal under test is identified, with its source.
- [ ] The baseline is a standard baseline, or the choice is argued.
- [ ] Baseline and method get the same tuning grid and effort.
- [ ] The target quality Q comes from the baseline, near the end of its budget.
- [ ] The prediction is numeric, and the pass rule and stop rules are clear.
- [ ] The evaluation text is sealed from tuning.

For a result:
- [ ] The pre-registration was public before the full runs (commit or pull request time).
- [ ] The result follows the pre-registration, or lists every deviation.
- [ ] R_FLOP and R_time are both reported, with the cost-to-quality curve.
- [ ] All seeds are reported, and the validity checks passed.
- [ ] The numbers in RESULT.md match the logs.
- [ ] The verdict and its scale are stated plainly, and the limits are honest.
